How it works

1
Semantic search
Your recipe is matched against 36,000+ substitution pairs from historical cookbooks using food-domain embeddings.
2
Grounded context
The most relevant substitution records — with book and year — are assembled as context.
3
LLM synthesis
An AI rewrites the recipe using the historical context as its source of truth, not general knowledge.
4
Cited sources
Every answer shows which books informed it — so you know where the substitution logic comes from.

Sources: Project Gutenberg & Internet Archive. All books pre-1929 public domain.
